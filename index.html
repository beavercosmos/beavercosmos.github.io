Questions

Good to know.

Do I have to take every service?

No. Many clients start with one video ad, then add ads management or WhatsApp automation once they see results.

What do you need to make a video ad?

Product photos, the price, and anything you want said: an offer, delivery info, your brand name. We plan the rest with you.

Will the voice sound robotic?

No. We choose natural, human-sounding narrators in English, Urdu, Arabic and other languages, and check every line before delivery.

Do you work with businesses outside Pakistan?

Yes. We work remotely with brands anywhere, and make ads in the language your customers speak.

How does the WhatsApp automation work?

We connect your business number to the official WhatsApp Business Platform. Customers get instant answers, see your products, and can place an order in the chat. You can step in any time.

Do I keep control of my pages and ad account?

Yes. Everything stays in your name. You give us access through Meta's own settings and can remove it whenever you want.

Who owns the final video?

You do. Use it on any platform, as often as you like.
